The Process

How AgriVision AI Works

A four-step pipeline from a single leaf photo to a farmer's next action.

01

Capture Leaf Photo

A farmer takes a photo of a crop leaf using any smartphone camera — no special equipment needed.

02

AI Diagnosis & Staging

Our deep learning model detects the disease and classifies it into Stage 1, 2, or 3 based on severity.

03

Crop & Weather Analysis

The system cross-references soil, weather, and historical crop-loss data to generate the season's crop recommendations.

04

Actionable Advisory

Farmers receive treatment steps, preventive tips, and crop suggestions in their own language via the app.
